Foundation-only continuous top-down 2D JRPG pixel art game map of HUECO MUNDO from BLEACH, a small playable stretch of its immense white desert under perpetual moonlit-night atmosphere. Canvas 6:5 landscape, orthographic overhead ground view with NO horizon and NO visible sky. Beautiful sophisticated 32-bit pixel clusters with restrained detail, broad coherent WHITE and pale silver sand dune flows, subtle blue-gray sweeping ripple ridges, cool deep but readable moonlit shadows, sparse areas of mineral grit and naturally eroded flat pale bedrock. Composition: a meandering broad open sand route from the upper left toward center and southeast, several larger quiet open encounter clearings connected by sand, with dune shapes directing the eye rather than a symmetric arena or grid. Most of the desert stays empty intentionally; preserve a sense of vast loneliness. No yellow daytime desert, cactuses, green vegetation, water or oasis. STRICTLY GROUND FOUNDATION ONLY: no trees or branches, large rocks, skull props, ruins sticking up, pillars, gates, walls, buildings, actors, monsters, text, symbols, UI, grid or labels. Future quartz dead trees and outcrops will be separate objects. Fine material transitions feel continuous and natural, no texture collage or isolated square patches, restrained lighting and no noisy all-over dithering.
